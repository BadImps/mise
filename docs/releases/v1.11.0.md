# Mise v1.11.0 — 2026-11-17

Catalogue expansion and publisher photographs.

- 109 new drinks from TheCocktailDB's public API, 66 Caribbean records (196 Flavors) and 147 meal records (TheMealDB), searchable offline in a new full-catalogue browser.
- Drinks shelf gained filters: alcohol (source-labelled), base spirit, and occasion (marked browsing suggestions).
- "Start mixing" used for drinks; "Start cooking" elsewhere.
- Recipe cards show their source's photographs: TheCocktailDB hot-link thumbnails; every bundled publisher recipe carries its own page photograph (previously many showed unrelated Wikimedia matches), and saved copies of those false positives are corrected when the app loads.
- Seven misleading cook-step tool photographs replaced with line art.
- Storage fixes: saving recipe 801 no longer evicts the oldest import; failed storage writes roll the kitchen back intact; failed bulk flush rolls back to the pre-batch snapshot.
- 14 new international pastries (empanada gallega, banitsa, samosa, sambousek, fatayer, sari burma, Brick à l'Oeuf, Cape Verde pastels, pastel Mandi'ó).
- ~400 publisher photographs backfilled from each record's own source page (hot-link, never bundled).
